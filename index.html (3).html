What the council is for.

  • Represents student opinions, concerns and ideas
  • Links students and school administration
  • Organises events, campaigns and initiatives
  • Coordinates clubs and student projects
  • Lets every student propose improvements and follow their progress

Structure and election rules are proposals until the school approves them.
